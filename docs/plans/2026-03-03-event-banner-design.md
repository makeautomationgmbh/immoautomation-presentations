# Event Banner Design — Roll-Up Pair

**Date:** 2026-03-03
**Format:** 2x Roll-Up Banners, 85 x 200 cm each
**Print resolution:** 300 DPI (2,551 x 6,004 px per banner)
**Digital mockup:** Proportional HTML at 850 x 2000 px

---

## Context

- Small conference, ~180 attendees (50% Makler, 50% industry)
- Two banners flanking a table with laptop demo + printed flyers
- Goals: Brand awareness + Lead generation (demo booking via QR → cal.com)
- Reusable across future events (no event-specific branding)
- White/light background, German language

---

## Content Decisions

### Headlines
- **H1:** "Social Media für Makler auf Knopfdruck."
- **H2:** "Ihr CRM hat die Daten. Wir machen den Rest. 60 Sekunden."

### KPIs (contrast metrics)
1. 45 Min → 1 Min (pro Post)
2. 5 Tools → 1 Plattform
3. 5+ Stunden/Woche gespart

### Post Mockups
Three real client posts (Wüstenrot, Sieger & Sieger, VR Immo-Service) displayed in fanned/stacked layout on Banner 2.

### CTA
QR code → dedicated event landing page (cal.com demo booking)
Text: "Demo buchen — kostenlos & unverbindlich"

---

## Banner 1 (Left) — "The Hook"

Top-to-bottom content zones:

1. **Logo** — immoautomation wordmark
2. **H1** — "Social Media für Makler auf Knopfdruck."
3. **H2** — "Ihr CRM hat die Daten. Wir machen den Rest. 60 Sekunden."
4. **KPI boxes** — Three contrast metrics in bold visual treatment
5. **Social media logos** — Instagram, Facebook, Google Business (large, colorful)
6. **Integration partners** — onOffice + Propstack logos
7. **Trust bar** — DSGVO-konform · Made in Austria · Hosting in DE

---

## Banner 2 (Right) — "The Proof + CTA"

Top-to-bottom content zones:

1. **Logo** — immoautomation wordmark (same position as Banner 1)
2. **Subheadline** — "Vom Exposé zum Post. Automatisch."
3. **Post mockups** — 3 real client posts (Wüstenrot, Sieger & Sieger, VR Immo-Service) in fanned stack with "Auto-generiert" and "60 Sekunden" badges
4. **QR code** — Large, prominent
5. **CTA text** — "Demo buchen — kostenlos & unverbindlich"
6. **Trust bar** — DSGVO-konform · Made in Austria · Hosting in DE

---

## Visual Style

- White background with subtle grid pattern
- immoautomation blue (#0080FF) as primary accent
- Inter font for all text, Newsreader italic for accent lines
- Sharp corners (0px border-radius) per brand guidelines
- Social media platform logos in their brand colors for recognition
